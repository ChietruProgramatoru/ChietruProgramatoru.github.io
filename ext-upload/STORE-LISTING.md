## Name

## Summary (short description, under 132 chars)
A calm new tab with a live countdown to Christmas, New Year or your own date, plus a simple focus timer.

## Description
Every new tab shows a live countdown to the day you're looking forward to: Christmas, New Year, or any date you choose (a trip, a birthday, a wedding).

What you get:
• Live days, hours, minutes and seconds countdown
• Count down to Christmas, New Year or your own named date
• Built-in focus timer (25/5, 50/10 or 15/3) with a gentle chime and a daily session count
• Light and dark themes, or follow your system
• Clock and date at a glance

Privacy: no account, no tracking, no network requests. Your settings are saved only in your own browser.

There's a small "buy the maker a coffee" link at the bottom. You can hide it in settings (⚙️ top right).
