Free video compressor

Make a video small enough for Discord, WhatsApp or email. It runs in your browser, so your video is never uploaded. Free, no watermark, no sign-up.

Heads-up: your browser re-records the video as it plays, so a 2-minute clip takes about 2 minutes. Keep this tab open and visible while it works.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. No paid tier, no download limit, no watermark. Your own device does the work, so it costs nothing to run. A coffee tip below is welcome but never required.

What format do I get?

MP4 in browsers that support it (recent Chrome, Edge and Safari), otherwise WebM. WebM plays in Chrome, Firefox, Discord and most phones.

Why didn't it hit my target size exactly?

Browser encoders aim for a bitrate, so the result can land a little above or below. If it's over, pick a smaller target or lower resolution and run it again.
