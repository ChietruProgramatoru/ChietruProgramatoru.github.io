# Amazon KDP puzzle books (paperback, free to publish)

KDP costs $0. Amazon prints each copy when it sells and pays ~60% of list price minus print cost, monthly, ~60 days after the month ends.
One-time setup (Petru): create a KDP account at kdp.amazon.com with your Amazon login, then fill in the Tax Interview and bank details. Claude fills in everything else.

Both books: Paperback, 8.5 x 11 in, black & white interior, white paper, **no bleed**, matte cover. Upload `interior.pdf` as manuscript and `cover.pdf` as "Upload a cover you already have".
Author / publisher name: Rokeru Puzzles. Low-content book: **No** (they have unique puzzle content, so they get a free ISBN; choose "Assign me a free KDP ISBN").
AI content question: answer **Yes, AI-generated text** for the cover and description copy (puzzles are generated by a program, not AI; cover art is drawn by code, no AI images). Honest answer, does not block publishing.
