50/30/20 budget calculator

The 50/30/20 rule splits take-home pay into 50% needs, 30% wants and 20% savings or debt payments. Change the split if it doesn't fit your life.

Optional: what you spend now, to compare.

What counts as what?

Needs

Rent or mortgage, utilities, groceries, insurance, minimum debt payments, transport to work.

Wants

Eating out, subscriptions, hobbies, shopping, holidays.

Savings

Emergency fund, retirement, investing, and extra debt payments above the minimum.
