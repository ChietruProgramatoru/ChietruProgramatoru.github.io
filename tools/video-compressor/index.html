FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. No paid tier, no file size cap, no watermark and no account. Your own device does the work, so it costs nothing to run. A coffee tip is welcome but never required.

Is it private?

Your video is read straight from your device and compressed there. Nothing is uploaded, and it keeps working if you go offline after the page loads.

How fast is it?

It uses the video encoder built into your computer or phone (the same one screen recorders use), so a minute of 1080p video usually takes well under a minute on a recent device. Keep the tab open until it finishes.

Which goal should I pick?

"Balanced" gives a much smaller file that still looks good. Use a size goal when an app has an upload limit: the tool works out the right bitrate from the video's length and, if the first try lands over the limit, automatically tries again a bit smaller.

Can I cut out part of the video?

Yes. Tap ✂️ Trim on a video, set the start and end, and it re-compresses just that part. Shorter clips also fit size limits at higher quality.

Which browsers work?

Recent Chrome, Edge, Safari (iPhone and Mac) and Firefox. If your browser is too old, the page tells you and uses a slower fallback.
