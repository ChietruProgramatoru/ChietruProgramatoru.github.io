Free image compressor

Shrink photos for email, websites and uploads. It runs entirely in your browser, so your images are never uploaded. Free with no limits, no watermark and no sign-up.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no paid tier and no download limit. The compressing happens on your own device, so it costs nothing to run. If it helped, a coffee tip below is appreciated but never required.

Are my photos private?

They never leave your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

Why is a PNG still big?

PNG is lossless. Switch the format to JPG or WebP for much smaller photos. Keep PNG for screenshots or images that need transparency.
